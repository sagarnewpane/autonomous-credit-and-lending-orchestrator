Most informal workers have no payslip. They do have records.

Dhago reads the records a borrower already leaves behind: wallet payments, remittances, cooperative sales and utility bills. It checks them against the income the borrower declares, applies written lending rules, estimates the risk and gives a loan officer a recommendation with its reasons.

Each stage appends an entry that carries the SHA-256 hash of the entry before it. Your browser is re-checking the hashes the pipeline wrote for this application. Try changing the log.

    Dhago began as team ZeroShot's entry to the GIBL AI/ML Hackathon 2026, organised by Global IME Bank, where it was one of 16 teams selected for the onsite round (3 to 5 July 2026, Budhanilkantha). It has since been rebuilt as a research prototype by Pujan Pant and Sagar Newpane.

    The model was trained on the hackathon's synthetic data, so this is not a lending product. Dhago recommends and a loan officer decides; it declines only when a written veto rule fires. The four applicants above are invented, and their numbers come from running the released code (version 0.2.0).
